# Acquisition Launchpad

Independent portfolio project based on private role research.

## Product

A multi-entity finance onboarding control plane. Working Phase 1: entity intake, chart-of-accounts mapping, opening-balance tie-outs, blockers, readiness, and sequenced onboarding. Planned later scope: intercompany eliminations, integration synchronization, first-close simulation, and full migration audit packet.

## Locked rules

- This is not a DualEntry product and must not imply affiliation with Entry Inc.
- Do not use DualEntry logos, private data, proprietary screenshots, or customer claims.
- Synthetic data only. Do not present generated ledgers as real companies or transactions.
- Deterministic accounting logic; AI may explain or suggest mappings but cannot approve them.
- Money uses integer cents or `Decimal`; debits and credits must tie exactly.
- Every import, mapping, exception, override, readiness result, and sequence decision is auditable.
- Backend: Python 3.13, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic.
- Frontend: the owner-selected Entity Control Center direction uses React, strict TypeScript, and Vite.
- Phase 1 must be fully working and tested before the project can be described as in progress.
- Ship tests, documentation, deterministic fixtures, accessibility, and browser proof.
- No resume edits, publishing, external deployment, application submission, or outreach without explicit approval.
